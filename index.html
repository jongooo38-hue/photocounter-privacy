Privacy Policy

Photo Counter Badge · Last updated: August 1, 2026

Summary: Photo Counter Badge does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything the app does happens entirely on your device. There are no ads and no analytics.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Photo Counter Badge application (the "App"), developed by PPUPPULabs ("we", "us", "our"), handles information. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

None. The App does not collect any personal or sensitive user data. We operate no servers, and the App makes no network connections to us. We cannot see how you use the App.

Photos and Folders

You choose a folder for the App to monitor. The App only counts how many image files are in that folder. It does not open, view the contents of, upload, copy, or transmit your photos. Your folder selection and the resulting count are stored only on your device.

Permissions

The App uses the following Android permissions solely to provide its features — never to collect or transmit your data:

  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): to show the floating badge on top of other apps.
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE): to keep the badge visible and the count up to date while you use your phone.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show the ongoing notification that the foreground service requires.

Purchases

The optional "Pro Unlock" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment information — it is handled by Google. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google processes payments.

Data Storage

App settings — such as which folders you selected, badge positions, and your Pro status — are stored locally on your device using Android's standard local storage. Uninstalling the App removes this data. Nothing is backed up to us or to any third party by the App.

Third Parties

The App integrates only with Google Play Billing (for the optional purchase). It contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics SDKs.

Children

The App is a general-purpose utility, is not directed to children, and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
